BACKGROUND
You are being asked to participate in a research study at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign. Before you decide, it is important for you to understand why the research is being done and what it will involve. Please take time to read the following information carefully. Ask us if there is anything that is not clear or if you would like more information. Take time to decide whether you want to volunteer to take part in this study. Your participation in this research is voluntary. If you decide to participate, you are free to withdraw at any time. The purpose of this study is to develop ways to better support people in active learning by interacting with an AI system. Participation in this study will help us identify and refine human-AI interaction strategies that promote deeper conceptual understanding, provide empirical evidence to inform the design of educational AI systems, and advance the development of AI learning tools that move beyond passive information delivery toward actively facilitating conceptual change, ultimately benefiting the broader population of self-directed learners.

STUDY PROCEDURE
The study procedures are (1) completing pre-study questionnaires about demographic information, prior knowledge, and learning-related beliefs, (2) reading an assigned article on a target concept and drawing an initial concept map, (3) engaging in structured discussion with the AI Study Peer and self-directed search, and (4) completing post-study questionnaires assessing learning outcomes and overall experience. The entire study will be conducted via Zoom, with the researcher present as a silent observer, and will last approximately 60 minutes.
 
RISKS
 The risks associated with this study are minimal. Because we will collect your contact information to schedule the Zoom appointment, there is a risk of loss of confidentiality of participating in the study. To protect your privacy, researchers will deidentify any study-related data s and store them confidentially. Only authorized research personnel will have access to your data. If you feel uncomfortable or upset at any time, you may stop participating and leave the study at any time.

 
BENEFITS
We cannot guarantee any direct benefits from participating in this study. However, potential benefits may include the opportunity to practice explaining complex topics, to strengthen your understanding of concepts that often underlie common misunderstandings, and to reflect more deeply on how knowledge and beliefs are formed.
